**Play:** [taosengine.com/flight](https://taosengine.com/flight) · **Pilot's Manual:** [taosengine.com/flight/manual](https://taosengine.com/flight/manual)

## About the game

Taos Flight is a flight simulator that runs in your web browser. You fly over the real, streamed Earth, and you can start from any airport in the world. It is built on the Taos Engine, a custom WebGPU game engine that draws the terrain, sky, clouds, water, fire and aircraft.

- **Aircraft:** the Cessna 208 Caravan, the F9F-5 Panther carrier jet, the Boeing 777-200ER with a glass cockpit, the Pegase 101 glider, and a C-130 Hercules air tanker.
- **Training:** guided lessons on takeoff, landing, the traffic pattern, stalls, engine failure, partial-panel flying, carrier landings, ring courses, air tanker missions and thermal soaring. Every landing gets a score.
- **Instruments and navigation:** working cockpit instruments, an autopilot (HDG, NAV, APR/ILS, GPS, ALT, VS), VOR/ADF radios, GPS flight plans, ATIS, ground proximity callouts and TCAS.
- **World and weather:** real sun, Moon and stars for any time and date; weather from clear skies to thunderstorms and fog; live METAR weather; wind, turbulence, icing, thermals, ridge lift and wake turbulence.
- **Live and shared skies:** real airliner traffic from ADS-B, multiplayer rooms with chat and proximity voice radio, a controller's radar scope, and wildfires that a whole room can fight together.
- **More:** a flight recorder with replay, failures and emergencies to practice, the USS Nimitz off San Diego, flights over the Moon and Mars, a ride on the ISS, and a free camera.

### Requirements

A desktop browser with WebGPU support (recent Chrome or Edge on Windows, macOS or ChromeOS; Safari on recent macOS and iOS) and a reasonably capable graphics card. A mouse works best; keyboard, gamepad and touch input also work.

## Reporting a bug

Before you open an issue, search the existing issues to see if someone has already reported it. A helpful report includes:

- What happened, and what you expected to happen
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- Your browser and version, operating system and GPU
- The aircraft, location (airport or place), mode and weather/time settings
- Screenshots, a video, or a saved flight recording (**Save** on the replay bar), if you have them
- Any errors in the browser's developer console (F12)
